Origami

Origami is a creative hobby where you fold paper into different shapes like animals, flowers, or objects. It is relaxing and helps improve focus, patience, and creativity.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Origami Paper
  • Flat Surface
  • Patience

Journaling

Journaling is a hobby where you write your thoughts, ideas, or daily experiences in a notebook. It helps improve self-expression, organization, and reflection.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Notebook or Journal
  • Pen or Pencil
  • Ideas or Thoughts

Air Dry Clay

Air dry clay is a creative hobby where people shape clay into figures, decorations, or small sculptures. The clay hardens naturally without baking, making it easy to use at home.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Air Dry Clay
  • Sculpting Tools
  • Flat Work Surface

Adult Colouring

Adult colouring is a relaxing hobby where you use markers or colored pencils to fill in detailed designs. It helps reduce stress and allows you to enjoy creative expression in a simple way.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Colouring Book
