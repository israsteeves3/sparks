Cross Stitch

Cross stitch is a relaxing hobby where you sew small X-shaped stitches on fabric to create patterns or images. It is a creative activity that helps improve focus and patience.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Embroidery Thread
  • Needle
  • Fabric and Embroidery Hoop

Harmonica

Playing the harmonica is a musical hobby where you blow and draw air through a small instrument to create melodies. It is easy to learn and great for relaxing or expressing creativity through music.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Harmonica
  • Music Guide or Song Sheet
  • Practice

Nail Art

Nail art is a creative hobby where you decorate and design fingernails using colors, patterns, and details. It allows you to express style and creativity through different nail designs.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Nail Polish
  • Nail File
  • Nail Art Brush or Tools

Tarot Reading

Tarot reading is a hobby where you use a deck of tarot cards to reflect, think, and explore ideas about life situations. It is often used for personal guidance, creativity, or self-reflection.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Tarot Card Deck
  • Guidebook
  • Crystals and Candles
