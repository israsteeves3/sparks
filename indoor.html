Diamond Art

Diamond art is a craft where small colorful beads are placed on a sticky canvas to create a sparkling picture. It is similar to paint-by-numbers and helps improve focus and creativity.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Diamond Beads
  • Applicator Pen
  • Adhesive Canvas Design

Scratch Art

Scratch art is a creative hobby where you use a wooden stylus to scratch the surface of special paper to reveal colorful designs underneath. It is relaxing and fun while allowing you to create detailed artwork.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Scratch Art Paper
  • Wooden Stylus
  • Creativity

Soap Making

Soap making is a hands-on hobby where you mix ingredients and pour liquid soap into molds to create decorative or scented soaps. It is a relaxing and creative activity that lets you customize colors, shapes, and fragrances.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Soap Base
  • Silicone Mold
  • Fragrance Oil

Tie Dye

Tie dye is a creative hobby where you twist or tie fabric and apply colorful dye to create unique patterns. It is fun and allows you to design personalized clothing or fabric items.

Items needed for hobby:

  • White Cotton Clothing
  • Fabric Dye
  • Rubber Bands

Candle Making

Candle making is a craft where you melt and pour wax, add fragrance or color, and create candles that are both functional and decorative.

Items needed for hobby:

  • Bees Wax
  • Wicks
  • Fragrance
